- ma_so_thue (STRING|NULL): MST doanh nghiep
- so_dien_thoai (STRING|NULL)
- email (STRING|NULL)
- dia_chi (STRING|NULL)
- website (STRING|NULL)
- nguoi_phu_trach_id (STRING|NULL): FK -> nhan_su.id (nguoi sale phu trach KH)
- ghi_chu (STRING|NULL)
- nguoi_tao_id (STRING|NULL): FK -> nhan_su.id
- ngay_tao (STRING ISO 8601)
- ngay_cap_nhat (STRING ISO 8601)
- trang_thai (STRING): hoat_dong | tam_dung | da_xoa

Indexes:

- trang_thai [asc] + ngay_tao [desc]
