Collection: nhan_su
Chua thong tin nguoi dung (users) - Chuong 3.4 Hethong.md

Fields:
- id (STRING, primary): UID tu Firebase Authentication (bat buoc)
- ma_nhan_vien (STRING): NV001, NV002...
- ho_va_ten (STRING): Ho va ten day du (bat buoc)
- so_dien_thoai (STRING|NULL): 090x...
- email (STRING): Email dang nhap Firebase (bat buoc, unique)
- chi_nhanh_id (STRING|NULL): FK -> collection chi_nhanh.id
- phong_ban_id (STRING|NULL): FK -> collection phong_ban.id
- chuc_vu (STRING|NULL): Quan ly, Truong phong, Nhan vien...
- vai_tro (STRING): quan_tri_he_thong | giam_doc | truong_phong | nhan_vien_kinh_doanh | nhan_vien_ky_thuat (bat buoc)
- url_anh_dai_dien (STRING|NULL): Firebase Storage URL
- trang_thai (BOOLEAN): TRUE = hoat dong, FALSE = khoa (khong cho dang nhap)
- ngay_tao (STRING ISO 8601): UTC ISO string
- ngay_cap_nhat (STRING ISO 8601): UTC ISO string

Indexes can:
- email [asc unique]
- chi_nhanh_id [asc] + trang_thai [asc]
- phong_ban_id [asc] + vai_tro [asc]
