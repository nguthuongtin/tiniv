Collection: bao_cao_cong_viec
Nhan vien bao cao ngay - Chuong 3.11

Fields:
- id (STRING, primary)
- ngay_bao_cao (STRING YYYY-MM-DD, required): Ngay lam bao cao
- nhan_vien_id (STRING, required): FK -> nhan_su.id
- du_an_id (STRING|NULL): FK -> ho_so_du_an.id
- cong_viec_id (STRING|NULL): FK -> cong_viec.id
- noi_dung_thuc_hien (STRING, required): Lam gi hom nay
- ke_hoach_ngay_mai (STRING|NULL): Ngay mai lam gi
- kho_khan (STRING|NULL): Gap kho khan gi
- ngay_tao (STRING ISO 8601): Thoi gian gui bao cao

Indexes:
- nhan_vien_id [asc] + ngay_bao_cao [desc]
- du_an_id [asc] + ngay_bao_cao [desc]
